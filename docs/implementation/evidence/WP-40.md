WP-40 was completed on 2026-11-19 as migration `0074_liquidity_forecasting.sql` (schema 74). It is local and synthetic.
It builds on the WP-24 signed liquidity evidence, the WP-37 governed warehouse and the WP-17 owned cases. See the

## Delivered behavior

1. **Reproducible forecasts.**
   - Each forecast stores four inputs: the liquidity observation, the warehouse watermark, the policy version and
     settlement outflow history at a recorded warehouse fact cut.
   - `finance.forecast_compute` is pure. Reproduction recomputes the result at the stored cut and records whether the
     digest matched.
2. **Confirmed funds only from fresh signed evidence.**
   - Stale, missing, invalid or unreachable evidence gives `UNCONFIRMED` / `DEGRADED`, with no confirmed funds.
   - Data-quality flags (`INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`, `WAREHOUSE_STALE`, `WAREHOUSE_NEVER_INGESTED`) make a forecast
     `UNCERTAIN`.
3. **Breach handling.**
   - A breach restricts new external submissions: the payment waits as `RAIL_PAUSED` / `LIQUIDITY_RESTRICTED`, with no
     hold and nothing sent.
   - It opens an owned `FINANCE` case in `finance-operations`. A degraded forecast escalates too. Open cases are reused
     within an episode.
   - A lift needs a Finance maker, a different Finance approver and a current confirmed within-limits forecast.
4. **Controlled automation.**
   - Only a confirmed breach proposes settlement prefunding, capped by the approved per-action limit.
   - A Finance maker confirms it and a different approver approves it, each while its forecast is current. A newer
     forecast supersedes undecided proposals.
   - Core books one `LIQUIDITY_PREFUNDING` batch after re-checking the approval, both people's live roles and the
     per-action and daily limits. A replay returns the same batch.
5. **Policies and backtests.**
   - Versioned, validated policies with maker/checker changes. An optional schedule lets the worker record forecasts.
   - Every forecast includes a rolling backtest against actual outflow, with a previous-day baseline, labelled local
     synthetic history only.
6. **Clients.** Staff portal **Liquidity forecast** and eleven staff routes.

## Boundaries

- **Known limits:**
  - only the single-payment external submission path is gated (not the batch rail, ATM or internal transfers);
  - one currency and one horizon day;
  - outflow-only history;
  - the provider-side prefunding movement is confirmed only by later evidence.
- **Out of scope:** real treasury feeds and limits; machine-learned models; other automated actions.
- **Synthetic treasury reserve account** (SF-009).
