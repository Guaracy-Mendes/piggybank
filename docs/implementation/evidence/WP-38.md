# WP-38 completion evidence

WP-38 was completed on 2026-11-18 as migration `0072_audiences_insights.sql` (schema 72). It has **one local
journey-partner simulator role** (`SIM_JOURNEY`), following the user's direction to build one local template where
nothing is selected. See the [specification](../audiences-journeys.md) and the
[acceptance README](../../../tests/acceptance/journeys/README.md). Real audience and journey partners remain pending
(DEC-05, DEC-06, DEC-09).

## Delivered behavior

1. **Permissioned segmentation with versioned provenance.**
   - Minimized boolean signals from WP-37 warehouse facts.
   - Versioned, digested segments: `savings_starter` (marketing) and `dormant_check_in` (new `SERVICE_OUTREACH` purpose).
   - Audiences record their fact cut, member digest and exclusion counts.
   - Members get per-audience pseudonymous tokens.
2. **Advisory insights.**
   - Bank-rule insights carry their rule, segment, audience and cut. Partner insights carry partner provenance.
   - The customer sees a plain "why" and an in-app route only, and can dismiss their own insights.
3. **Withdrawal handling.** Consent is checked at build, export, delivery, receipt and display. Withdrawal:
   - hides suggestions;
   - withholds delivery;
   - suppresses new advice;
   - excludes the member from new exports.

   All records stay as immutable evidence.
4. **Journey partner (advice only).**
   - Signed export fetch and recommendations, with stored replayable responses and 409 on id reuse.
   - Approval, entitlement and limit fields, unlisted codes and routes, markup and unknown tokens are rejected and
     recorded.
   - The only possible effect is an advisory insight.
5. **Outcome reports.**
   - Explicit denominators and outcomes, each with a stated measure.
   - Dataset watermarks, and `MISSING_SOURCE`, `STALE_WATERMARK` and `LATE_DATA` flags. Immutable and digested.
6. **Clients.**
   - Staff portal: **Audiences & journeys**.
   - Customer portal: **Suggestions and notices** on Notifications.
   - Partner channel: two signed routes.
