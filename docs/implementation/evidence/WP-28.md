# WP-28 completion evidence

WP-28 was completed on 2026-11-17 as migration `0065_security_lifecycle.sql` (schema 65), within its local synthetic scope. The [specification](../security-lifecycle.md), [threat model](../../security/threat-model.md), [incident runbook](../../runbooks/security-incident.md) and [acceptance manifest](../../../tests/acceptance/security/README.md) give the details.

## Delivered behavior

1. **Recovery (FR-IAM-06, SEC-03).**
   - Customers can sign out everywhere.
   - Staff-assisted recovery records ownership verification and requires an onboarded customer.
   - Risk signals: contact changed within 7 days, open monitoring alert, recovery within 30 days. High risk or a credential reset needs a security approver independent of the requester and the customer.
   - Execution runs on the issuer through a least-privilege service client whose secret is re-read from a file. It revokes all sessions and, for a reset, sets `UPDATE_PASSWORD`; staff never handle credentials.
   - A safe security notice is sent, and transitions are immutable, recorded in history and audited.
2. **Protected secrets (SEC-02).** Server-held session tokens are sealed with AES-256-GCM, bound to their row and column, from a rotatable key ring (resolves SF-001). The executor secret rotates without restart.
3. **Privacy (AT-16).** A `RESTRICTION` request stops marketing and analytics use, and preferences cannot override it. An `EXPORT` request covers the subject's own personal data, with evidence content restricted. Both are processed under the existing retention controls.
4. **Access recertification (FR-CTRL-06, SEC-10).** Snapshot reviews with no self-review, immediate revocation and final decisions.
5. **Findings register (SEC-07, SEC-10).** Independent disposition. High and critical findings cannot be accepted and are counted as release-blocking.
6. **Sensitive-data inspection (AT-19, SEC-05).** Service logs from a complete journey contain none of the seeded credentials, tokens, secret or personal data.
7. **Clients and documents.**
   - Customer portal: **Sign out everywhere** and new privacy request options.
   - Staff portal: **Security** workspace.
   - Documents: sanitized threat model, incident runbook with a tabletop record.
   - Tooling: `make test-security`.

## Failures before the final run

These are retained here, not hidden:
- **Name clash.** A PL/pgSQL variable shadowed a column (`purpose`) in the privacy processor; renamed.
- **Overwritten schema.** A new OpenAPI schema name, `RecoveryInput`, overwrote the existing payment-recovery schema and broke the staff portal's types. The original was restored, the new schema renamed to `SecurityRecoveryInput`, and every new schema name checked against the committed contract.
- **Dark-mode contrast.** The first browser run exposed an existing contrast defect: the Profile card had a hard-coded light background in dark mode. Fixed with dark-theme overrides.
- **Module name collision.** Two modules named `identity` collided in the security test path; the issuer fixtures are now loaded by file.
- **Clippy.** A test had a redundant branch.
