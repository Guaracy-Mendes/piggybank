## Delivered behavior

1. **Assisted sessions (FR-CH-03).**
   - Staff with `assisted_service` open a session for a named customer, with channel, verification method and
     purpose.
   - The customer authorizes it in their own bank channel within 10 minutes and can end it at any time.
   - The session actor keeps the staff identity and names the represented customer and the channel.
2. **Channel policy (FR-CH-04).**
   - An immutable fixture sets verification methods, operations and session length per channel.
   - Only read and protective freeze are enabled; payments are enabled in no channel until DEC-09.
3. **Common rules.**
   - Every assisted decision goes through Controls: live session, matching parties, confirmed operation, the
     customer's own account and the account's restriction flags.
   - A protective freeze is the ordinary Core restriction, so the customer's own payments fail consistently.
4. **Recorded authority.**
   - Every decision is appended to an immutable record with its reason.
   - Session transitions are kept in history, and opening and authorization are audited.
   - Authorization audit records carry both the staff actor and the represented customer.
5. **Clients.**
   - Staff portal: **Assisted service**.
   - Customer portal: **Assistance requests** on **Connected apps & sharing**.
