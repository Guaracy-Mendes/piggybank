# WP-05 — Entitlement and approval evidence

Verified locally on 2026-10-23. Warning-free Rust/client checks and three real PostgreSQL integration suites passed. Real-issuer, Chromium and ingress checks passed after introducing the action-bound reauthentication route.

Implemented server-resolved canonical person links, current role/scope/expiry/revocation checks, authoritative resource-context authorization, independent role proposals/approvals, audited role revocation and deny-by-default trust boundaries. Token role strings do not grant resource access. Tests prove ownership checks, staff relationship plus scoped permission, customer denial of privileged administration, revocation, and rejection of maker self-approval through an alias linked to the same human.

Approval challenges bind a durable operation, actor/person, complete payment-detail fingerprint, unpredictable nonce and expiry. A browser reauthentication request uses the existing PKCE callback with forced login and an action-bound nonce. The callback checks the expected subject/issuer, nonce and fresh issuer authentication time before recording an immutable proof. Proof consumption is scoped and idempotent to one operation, with current authorization checked again.

Tests reject absent proof, changed amount, another operation, current account restriction and stale challenge. The real issuer flow rejects a different authenticated person, records the correct person's action-bound proof and rejects an expired challenge. Equivalent retry does not create another approval. Identity-proof receipt and Controls approval are separate recoverable commits rather than an asserted distributed transaction.

Authoritative account context is supplied through a non-request-deserialized domain port; Core integration occurs with account implementation. Staff administration screens, partner delegation, real product policy, stronger production authenticators and recovery qualification remain their planned packages. These foundations do not expose a functioning payment execution endpoint yet.
