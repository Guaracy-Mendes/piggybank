# WP-33 completion evidence

WP-33 was completed on 2026-11-18 as migration `0067_partner_consents.sql` (schema 67), as a **local synthetic R2
capability**. See the [specification](../partner-consents.md) and the
[acceptance README](../../../tests/acceptance/partners/README.md). Production enablement still requires the R1
launch gate (WP-32), real partner contracts and policy (DEC-06, DEC-09, DEC-11).

## Delivered behavior

1. **Registered partner clients (FR-IAM-05).**
   - A separate `partners` issuer realm issues client-credentials tokens with the `bank-partner` audience.
   - A token is accepted only for a client registered and ACTIVE in the bank registry.
   - Registration needs maker/checker approval. Suspension is immediate, and an audited history is kept.
2. **Customer consent (FR-IAM-05, FR-EXT-02).**
   - Scoped requests are bounded by the client's registration.
   - The customer authorizes in a bank channel within 15 minutes, choosing their own accounts.
   - Expiry and revocation are prospective, and Controls re-checks the live grant at every decision.
   - Non-disclosing errors throughout: 403 for consent problems, 404 for resources outside the grant.
3. **Versioned partner contract (FR-EXT-02).**
   - Every call needs a version header: a sunset version returns 410 with `Sunset`, and a deprecated version is
     served with `Deprecation` and `Sunset`.
   - The ingress boundary keeps these protocol headers through the safe error envelope.
4. **Quota and callbacks.**
   - Per-client per-minute quota, returning 429 with `Retry-After`.
   - HMAC-SHA256 signed consent callbacks are delivered by the worker with backoff. Each client's secret is shown
     once and sealed at rest.
5. **Partner-initiated payments (AT-21).**
   - Partners can quote and draft but never approve. The customer approves with action-bound step-up.
   - On confirmation the customer is recorded once as `authorized_actor`, so revocation cannot strand an approved
     payment. The partner remains the immutable initiator.
6. **Delegated authority (FR-ACC-08).**
   - Delegate references, then VIEW or PAY-up-to-a-limit grants with fixed terms.
   - The limit is enforced on the exact amount at quote, intent and confirmation. Delegates can never service the
     account.
   - Owner revocation or delegate renunciation takes effect immediately, while history and the delegate's past
     actor evidence are preserved.
7. **Clients.**
   - Customer portal: **Connected apps & sharing**, including the consent authorization view.
   - Staff portal: **Partners & consent** (registry, consents, callback deliveries).

## Failures before the final run

These are kept here, not hidden:
- **Callback URL rule.** The callback URL check rejected the test's one-digit loopback port (the rule requires 2–5
  digits). The test now uses port 19.
- **Stranded approved payments.** The first full acceptance run found that a customer-approved, partner-drafted
  payment never settled after revocation, because execution re-checked the revoked partner grant. Fixed with the
  write-once `authorized_actor`, which makes the customer the execution actor once they approve.
- **Partner replay.** A same-key partner replay tried to start a customer approval challenge. Partner replays now
  return the draft unchanged.
- **Dropped protocol headers.** The ingress safe envelope dropped `Sunset` on the 410 response. It now keeps a
  fixed allowlist of protocol headers.
- **Test assumptions.** The test's history query used `from` equal to `to` (Core requires `from < to`), and its
  header lookup was case-sensitive.
- **Browser findings.**
  - axe found staff checkbox targets under 24 px; fixed with `.check` styling.
  - The confirmation notice vanished on navigation because the page remounted; fixed by keeping the view mounted.
  - An ambiguous "Account" label matched two elements; the select now has an explicit label.

## Boundaries

- **Partners.** The partner clients are synthetic issuer fixtures. There are no real partner contracts,
  certification, mTLS or production egress controls, and callbacks go to loopback only.
- **Partner scope.** Partners can neither add recipients nor approve payments. Callbacks cover consent lifecycle
  only.
- **Delegation scope.** Delegation is person-to-person on a single account. Business mandates and multi-signature
  rules need policy.
