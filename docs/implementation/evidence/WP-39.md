## Delivered behavior

1. **Versioned route and approval policies.**
   - `payments.route_policies` holds immutable, validated versions. Version 1 is `PRIMARY_FALLBACK` over A then B,
     with health thresholds, fees and a minimum shadow sample.
   - Changes are maker/checker: a payment operator proposes, and an independent payment approver decides.
   - A version can become ACTIVE only after enough shadow decisions, unless it was active before. This makes rollback to
     a previously active version possible without a new shadow period.
2. **Measured provider health.** Health is computed per provider from recorded simulator outcomes over the policy's
   window: decisions, settled, rejected, non-executed, unknown outcomes and average acknowledgement latency. Version 1
   (`PRIMARY_FALLBACK`) uses a fixed order. A health-scored policy ranks providers instead: a provider with too few
   samples scores 0, and one above its unknown or non-execution threshold scores -1. Every decision records its
   policy version, rule, health scores and the shadow policy's choice.
3. **Safe fallback only on proven non-execution.**
   - Core owns the route log (`core.external_routes`). A re-route requires signed REJECTED evidence from the current
     provider, with a non-execution reason (`PROVIDER_CAPACITY_NOT_EXECUTED` or `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE_NOT_EXECUTED`).
   - It also requires an active submitted hold and an untried provider. Each piece of evidence can be used once.
   - The operation id, amount and hold are unchanged, so the economic identity is preserved. Finality checks the
     provider named in the latest route.
   - An unknown outcome (timeout, no response) never re-routes: it is inquired with the same provider.
4. **Shadow evaluation.** The shadow report gives decisions, the agreement rate, active and shadow choices per
   provider, fees and measured health. Its baseline is labelled `LOCAL_SIMULATOR_ONLY`.
5. **Clients.**
   - Staff portal: **Payment routing**, showing health, policy versions, changes, decisions and the shadow report.
   - Staff API: three routes under `/api/v1/staff/routing`.
   - Simulator: provider B serves `/rails-b/*` with its own signing key and scenarios, including `REJECT_CAPACITY`.
