# WP-21 — Durable simulated outgoing submission

Implemented and verified in the synthetic local environment on 2026-10-28. The new `bank-rails` protocol/adapter and persistent simulator implement SIMULATED/TST outgoing submission. Quotes bind the verified recipient, approved effective rail policy, source product/pricing, exact principal/fee, UTC date and original payment identity. The approved local calendar is explicitly synthetic UTC 24/7. No real payment network has been integrated.

Core reserves exact principal plus fee and gross daily principal usage atomically before possible provider work. The shared budget counts active external reservations and posted internal payments under the payer lock. Releasing an unsubmitted reservation frees that budget; replaying its released identity cannot reserve again. Submitted holds cannot be released by expiry, a caller-supplied evidence UUID, generic Core release/transfer commands or an orchestration status. Generic hold commands serialize with reservation binding and recheck the financial domain owner after the lock. Runtime principals still cannot directly edit financial tables.

Payments saves a stable provider instruction and short fenced work lease before HTTP. It marks Core submitted before a call can escape. No transaction is held during transport. A response recorder takes the same lock order and validates its lease/fence after waiting. Accepted-but-response-lost execution recovers through inquiry of the original instruction. A provider process restart retains its deduplication state. A crash after Core submission marking but before Payments work commits reconstructs the original provider instruction and inquires without resubmitting. NOT_FOUND remains uncertain and does not authorize release. Exhausted work has an owned payment exception.

Provider acknowledgments remain pending with no journal batch. Booked balances stay unchanged and held funds reduce availability by the exact total. Authentication uses a separate HMAC key, live timestamp checks and exact typed identity/amount/digest/date validation. Altered duplicate instructions conflict. Forged/wrong-key packets and signed altered amounts fail verification. Cancellation tombstones prevent delayed submission from executing an already cancelled identity. Bank-side cancellation authority and financial settlement/release are subsequent lifecycle work.

Operators can immediately pause new submissions. The policy row lock serializes pause with a new attempt. Existing inquiries continue during a pause. Resume requires an independent canonical approver, exact policy/revision binding and current maker/checker grants; a same-human login alias cannot approve, and revocation invalidates unused approval. Reapplication of a completed resume is idempotent and cannot undo a later pause. Policy versions are immutable, independently approved and auditable. The initial approved effective synthetic policy enables the fixture; later policy publications preserve an emergency pause.

The real browser flow verifies the recipient, displays exact SIMULATED payment details, performs issuer reauthentication and explicit confirmation, loses the provider response, then shows acknowledgment as pending. The displayed ledger balance, held amount and available balance are exact. Separate reviewer and approver sessions pause, propose, approve and apply resume through the workforce portal. Desktop/mobile screenshots were inspected and accessibility/overflow checks passed. An initial test incorrectly attempted a different legal identity for an already onboarded subject; the payment test now uses the customer's existing account. A rail-selector test locator was also corrected to use its accessible combobox role.
