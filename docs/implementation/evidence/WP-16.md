# WP-16 — Exact terms and acceptance evidence

Verified locally on 2026-10-23 with twelve real PostgreSQL integration tests, forward migrations and warning-free Rust/generated-client checks. Documents owns immutable synthetic terms versions and exact UTF-8 text digests, governed by independent product-administration approval. Terms must fit the published product's effective dates. Publication advances versions under a product lock; acceptance validates the current effective version under the same lock.

Acceptance binds verified issuer/subject, application, product, exact terms/digest, original channel and database time. Repeated acceptance returned the same reference. Missing approval, changed publication content, a foreign customer's acceptance lookup and another application's evidence validation failed. Publishing a later version preserved the earlier text/evidence and required renewed acceptance; expired terms were unavailable for new acceptance. Direct runtime deletion and retrospective owner mutation were rejected. Terms and acceptance facts commit in the owned Documents outbox.

A PostgreSQL PL/pgSQL name ambiguity discovered by the real acceptance test was corrected with a separate forward migration; historical applied migrations remain unchanged. Customer and staff journey integration follows WP-15/WP-18/WP-19. The exact retained text is stored in the owned database; statement files/object retention follow WP-25. These synthetic texts are not approved legal terms.
